# Signals checklist: early vs late stage

Use as a scoring sheet, not a trading signal. Score each item 0 (absent), 1 (partly), 2 (clearly present).

## Early-stage signals
- [ ] Core cost curve collapsing (compute, bandwidth, storage, inference, genome sequencing)
- [ ] Builders and researchers moving in before capital does
- [ ] Product is used despite being clunky
- [ ] Infrastructure ("picks and shovels") being built
- [ ] Mainstream commentators dismiss it as a toy
- [ ] Very low public awareness; few dedicated funds

## Late-stage signals
- [ ] Companies rename or rebrand to attach to the trend
- [ ] Non-investors ask how to get in; media coverage is saturated
- [ ] "This time is different" is the standard reply to valuation concerns
- [ ] Leverage growing: margin debt, structured products, circular financing between suppliers and customers
- [ ] Valuations rest on revenue far in the future rather than current profits
- [ ] IPO / token / SPAC issuance surges; quality of issuers falls
- [ ] Prominent skeptics capitulate
- [ ] Insiders sell while retail inflows peak
- [ ] Central bank or regulator warnings (e.g. Greenspan, Dec 1996: three years before the Nasdaq top)

## Reading the score
- Mostly early signals + strong fundamentals: displacement or early boom
- Mix: boom, still has room but fragile
- Mostly late signals: euphoria; timing the end remains unreliable (see 1996-2000)
