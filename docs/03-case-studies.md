# Case studies (first pass)

Each case should eventually have: timeline, peak-to-trough, what survived, who profited, Perez stage, signals scored.

| Episode | Years | Trigger | Peak-to-trough (headline) | What lasted |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Roaring Twenties / 1929 crash | 1921-1932 | Radio, autos, margin credit | Dow ~-89% (1929-32) | Securities regulation, SEC |
| Nifty Fifty | 1968-1974 | "One-decision" blue-chip growth stocks at very high P/E | S&P ~-48% (1973-74 bear) | Lesson: great companies can be bad investments at any price |
| Japan asset bubble | 1986-1990 | Easy credit, land and equity inflation | Nikkei ~-80% over the following two decades | Deflation and balance-sheet recession playbook |
| Dot-com | 1995-2002 | Netscape, commercial internet | Nasdaq ~-78% (Mar 2000 to Oct 2002) | Amazon, Google, broadband, e-commerce |
| US housing / credit | 2003-2009 | Cheap credit, securitisation | S&P ~-57% (Oct 2007 to Mar 2009) | Dodd-Frank, QE |
| Crypto cycles | 2010-present | Bitcoin, ICOs, DeFi, NFTs | Bitcoin has fallen ~80% or more in several cycles | Spot ETFs, stablecoins, tokenisation experiments |
| Meme / SPAC / liquidity | 2020-2021 | Zero rates, stimulus, retail apps | Many SPACs and speculative growth stocks -80%+ | Retail participation, options flows |
| AI wave | 2022-present | ChatGPT, GPU demand | Open question | Open question |

Figures are approximate and should be verified from the series in `data/market/` once downloaded.

## Open research question: where is AI in Perez's cycle?
Arguments for "still in frenzy" (valuation concentration, circular financing between chip, cloud and lab players, heavy capex ahead of revenue) and for "real deployment already underway" (rapid enterprise adoption, large measurable usage) both exist. This repo should track evidence on both sides over time rather than pick one.
